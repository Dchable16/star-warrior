🎤 My Speech Helper

"Hello everyone! My name is Diego Matías and today I want to present my spaceship: Star Warrior."

/ Je-lóu éb-ri-uan! Mái néim is Diego Matías and tu-déi Ái uónt tu pre-sént mái spéis-xip: Star Uór-i-or. /

"Inside, it has a control panel, space seats, a rest area, a science lab, and a life support system with oxygen tanks."

/ Ín-said, it jas a con-tróul pá-nel, spéis síits, a rest éi-ria, a sái-ens lab, and a láif su-pórt sís-tem uid ók-si-yen tanks. /

"Outside, it has an aerodynamic nose cone, powerful thrusters, stabilizer fins, observation windows, and solar panels."

/ Áut-said, it jas an e-ro-dai-ná-mic nóus cón, páu-er-ful zrás-ters, stei-bi-lái-zer fins, ob-ser-véi-shon uín-dous, and sóu-lar pá-nels. /

"Its main missions are to navigate and orbit around the Earth, launch and repair satellites, and visit other planets."

/ Its méin mí-shons ar tu ná-vi-gueit and ór-bit a-ráund di érz, lonch and ri-péar sá-te-laits, and ví-sit ó-der plá-nets. /

"Thank you very much! Any questions?"

/ Zank iu vé-ri mach! É-ni kués-chons? /
